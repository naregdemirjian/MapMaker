paleodem-1deg.bin holds the PALEOMAP Paleodigital Elevation Models (PaleoDEMs) for the Phanerozoic:

  Scotese, C. R., and Wright, N. (2018). PALEOMAP Paleodigital Elevation Models (PaleoDEMS) for the
  Phanerozoic. PALEOMAP Project. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5460860

licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for Map Maker: the 1 x 1 degree grids (109 maps, 0 to 540 million years ago) resampled to
one-degree cells (each the mean of the four grid points at its corners) and rounded to whole metres
(scripts/paleodem-extract.py), then packed (scripts/pack-paleodem.ts).
